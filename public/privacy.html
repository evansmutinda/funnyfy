1. Who we are

FunnyFy is an AI image generation app operated by EMK Studios. The App lets you upload a photo, choose a style, and generate stylized images. Subscriptions are sold through Google Play and managed with RevenueCat.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account with your name, phone number, or postal address. We collect and process the following categories of information. These categories are intended to align with Google Play Data safety disclosures (for example: Photos, Identifiers, Usage Data, Diagnostics).

2.1 Photos you upload

When you generate content, you upload a photo (often including a face). That photo is processed to create images. Photos are personal data. We send them to our AI and content-moderation partners solely to provide the service.

Face images / biometric note: FunnyFy does not extract, compute, or store facial geometry, face templates, biometric embeddings, or similar biometric identifiers on our systems. We pass the raw image (or a temporary image reference) through to third-party processors (for example Replicate for generation and Sightengine for moderation) to deliver the feature you requested, and we do not build a face-recognition database. Those processors handle the image under their own terms and privacy policies. If laws such as Illinois BIPA, Texas CUBI, or GDPR special-category rules apply to your use, this pass-through processing model is an important part of how the App works — and you should only upload photos you are allowed to use.

2.2 Anonymous account identifier

On first launch we create a random user ID (UUID) and store it on your device and on our servers. This ID lets us authenticate API requests, track usage quotas, and link your subscription. It is not your legal name.

2.3 Subscription and purchase information

If you subscribe, Google Play and RevenueCat process payment. We receive subscription status, product/tier, renewal or expiration timing, and a RevenueCat / Play-related user identifier. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Email: The App does not ask for your email. If Google Play or RevenueCat provides an email address associated with your purchase, we may store it on your user record for support and account matching.

2.4 Usage and quota data

We record how many generations you use in a billing period so we can enforce your plan limits and operate the service.

2.5 Technical and security data

When the App talks to our servers, we may process:

  • IP address (rate limiting, abuse prevention, security logs)
  • User-Agent and similar client metadata (app/OS/device context in security and diagnostics logs)
  • Device / OS / app version information used for crash reporting and debugging

2.6 Crash and error reports

If crash reporting is enabled (e.g. Sentry), we may receive technical crash data tied to your anonymous user ID, plus device and app context. We configure crash reporting to avoid sending unnecessary personal details (for example, we do not enable default PII collection in Sentry).

2.7 Content saved on your device

Images you save to your device (for example in a FunnyFy album) stay on your device under your control. That local gallery is not uploaded again unless you choose to use a photo for a new generation.

2.8 What we do not collect

We do not collect GPS or device location for location-based features. We do not use the App to serve third-party advertising networks. We do not require contacts, SMS, or microphone access for core generation.

3. Legal bases for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)): processing needed to provide the App features you request — including uploading a photo, generating images, authenticating your session, and enforcing your subscription quota.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): security, fraud and abuse prevention, rate limiting, debugging, and service improvement, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must retain or disclose information to comply with law (for example tax, accounting, or lawful requests).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): where required for a specific optional processing activity; you may withdraw consent without affecting processing already carried out lawfully on another basis.

Where special-category data rules could be argued to apply because a photo shows a face, we rely on the fact that processing is limited to providing the service you request (pass-through generation/moderation as described above) and, where required by law, on applicable exceptions or consent mechanisms. Contact us if you have questions about the basis that applies to you.

The App uses third-party AI to transform your uploaded photo into stylized output. Uploaded images may also be checked by automated moderation tools to block disallowed content.

AI processing is performed to deliver the feature you requested, not to build advertising profiles. Questions about AI processing: funnyfyapp@gmail.com.

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or other lawful bases) offered by those providers.

8. Data retention

We do not operate FunnyFy as a long-term personal photo library. Retention in practice:

  • Uploaded photos / job image references: used for generation and moderation. We do not extract or keep facial embeddings. Temporary image data and job records may remain associated with a generation job while that job is processed and for a limited operational period afterward; partner-hosted outputs (for example on Replicate) are typically short-lived under the partner’s own expiry rules. We do not promise a single fixed deletion clock in this policy because cleanup depends on job status and provider behaviour — you may request deletion of account-linked data (see section 10).
  • Account / UUID and usage records: kept while you use the App; deleted or anonymized on verified request where we are not legally required to keep them.
  • Security and rate-limit data (IP, User-Agent, related events): kept as reasonably necessary for abuse prevention and security. Short-lived rate-limit counters are purged on an ongoing basis; security event logs are not marketed as a permanent archive and may be removed or reduced over time as part of normal operations.
  • Subscription and billing-related records: retained as needed for accounting, tax, chargebacks, and legal compliance (often several years).
  • Crash reports: retained according to our crash-reporting provider’s settings and our operational needs.

You may ask us to delete personal data we hold; see section 10.
