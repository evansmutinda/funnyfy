When a user hits their photo generation quota early, you must balance revenue generation with user retention. The best approach is a hybrid model offering immediate, flexible paths to get more generations. [1] 
Here is how to handle maxed quotas effectively:
## 1. Implement Instant Add-Ons (Top-Ups)

* One-time packs: Offer small, affordable credit packs (e.g., 50 photos for $2).
* Frictionless purchase: Place a "Buy More" button directly on the generation screen.
* No expiration: Let top-up credits roll over to the next month so users feel they get full value. [2] 

## 2. Provide Value-Exchange (Free Options)

* Rewarded ads: Let users watch a 30-second video ad to earn 1 to 3 free photo generations.
* Social sharing: Grant bonus credits if they post an app-generated photo to Instagram or TikTok with your watermark/tag.
* Referral program: Give credits to both the user and a friend when the friend downloads the app. [3, 4, 5] 

## 3. Encourage Plan Upgrades

* Pro-rated upgrades: Allow users to move to a higher tier immediately by only paying the price difference.
* Reset cycle: Restart their 30-day billing cycle immediately upon upgrading to the higher tier.
* Tier highlighting: Show them exactly how much they would save per photo by upgrading instead of buying top-ups. [6, 7, 8] 

## 4. Optimize the UX & Messaging

* Advance warning: Show a progress bar when they hit 80% and 90% of their limit so the hard stop isn't a surprise.
* Clear countdown: When the limit is reached, display the exact date and time their quota resets.
* Empathetic copy: Frame the notification positively (e.g., "You've been creative today! Your next free refill arrives in 4 days. Want to keep going right now?"). [9] 

## 5. Offer "Slow Mode" (Alternative)

* Throttled speed: Instead of a hard block, let them keep generating for free but move their requests to a low-priority queue that takes 5–10 minutes per image.
* Pay to skip: Offer a cheap option to skip the wait queue.
